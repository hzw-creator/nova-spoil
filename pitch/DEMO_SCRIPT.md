# Demo script (2 minutes) — Owner: Agent B (draft; polish at M3)

**Props:** 6–12 brown/spotty bananas, 1 moldy or badly bruised avocado (or a printed photo), 1 loaf of bread.
**Setup:** laptop running `npm run dev`, `/stage` fullscreen on the projector, `.env.local` has the key. Press **R** right before going on.

| Time | Who / action | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Speaker 1, slide | "Restaurants run on 3–5% margins, and a big chunk of what they buy goes in the trash. Uber has surge pricing. We built **spoil pricing** for DoorDash." |
| 0:15 | Hold bananas to the camera, press **Space** | "This café has bananas that will be garbage by tomorrow. Watch." |
| 0:20 | Point at the timeline | "The vision model rates freshness. Our forecast says 8 of these won't sell in time. The agent picks a dish from the kitchen's real recipes and prices it with code, not vibes." |
| 0:35 | Phone pings | "And it's live on the DoorDash app: Warm Banana Bread, today only." |
| 0:45 | Press **P** | "Customers nearby see it… orders roll in… and that number is money that would've been in the trash." |
| 1:05 | Hold the moldy avocado, press **Space** | "Now the sad path. Moldy avocado. The agent **refuses**: food safety is a hard rule in code, not a prompt. It discards it and lowers next week's order." |
| 1:25 | Replay view | "Same week, same customers, with and without the agent: **−X% waste, +Y points of margin.** Simulated, but every number is computed, not made up." |
| 1:45 | Close | "Every DoorDash restaurant has a fridge. Spoil pricing turns it into a revenue stream." |

**Backup plan:** camera fails → press **M** (mock scan). Wifi/API fails → mock scan still works offline. Everything fails → play the backup video recorded at 16:40.
